## Direction contract

THESIS: The screen is a number plate, not a dashboard. One bus, one state, one time — set in giant light lowercase on a light plate, with the tracking state as a solid PU-blue field you can read from across a bus stop. It refuses the category default of a card-and-header map app where the state hides in a badge.

OWN-WORLD: PU blue on a light plate. Cool-grey ground (#F6F7FB) with near-black ink (#0F1B2D). One committed colour, PU blue (#0A76D6 — taken from the university's own app screen the owner supplied on 12 Sep 2026) with white ink on it, carries the live state, the primary action, every selection flood, the route line and the whole app bar. Waiting states are white fields with a 2px ink outline; fault states (offline, conflict, GPS lost) are crimson (#AE2338, the reference's error red) with white ink; ended is a 10% ink wash. Inputs are white boxes with a 2px slate (#7F8DA3) outline that turns crimson when invalid; errors are set in crimson text under the box. No radius, no shadows, no gradients, no card chrome — every shape is unchanged from the Metro world; only the ink changed. Type is self-hosted Work Sans: headlines at 200 weight, lowercase, 4–6rem, allowed to run off the right edge; body 400 at 1.125rem; times and ages in tabular numerals. Icons are lucide, one stroke. Nav is the Metro app bar on a solid PU-blue field: white circled icons, the active one a white disc with a blue glyph; bottom on phones, left rail on desktop. The round Parul University badge (public/logo.png, cut from the owner's screenshot) with the name "PU Transit" and the tagline "Find your bus. Every day." live in that blue band — a 56px strip above the page on phones, the rail top on desktop — never as an eyebrow inside a page. Focus rings take currentColor (ink on the plate, white on blue). The basemap is OpenFreeMap positron so the blue route line and blue live stamp are the only saturated marks on the map.

STORY: A student at a roadside understands in one glance that bus 14 is live (blue), waiting (outlined) or gone (red/grey), how old that truth is, and which stops come next; they trust it because the screen never says "live" without a fresh sample. A driver sees a checklist, presses one giant blue tile to start, and can leave the phone alone. An admin pivots between fleet, routes, users and system with giant lowercase pivot headers and sees every consequence spelled out before an irreversible action.

FIRST VIEWPORT (student, 390×844): blue brand band on top (badge left, "PU Transit" and the tagline beside it). Headline "find your bus" at 4rem light, two lines, clipped at the right edge. Below it, three shift tiles in a row (first / adm-medical / general), the chosen one flooded blue. Under them a full-width white input "bus number" with a blue "search" tile-button beside it — the primary action, above the fold. Results appear as a stack of outlined tiles; choosing one floods it blue and replaces the headline with "bus 14" clipped, then the state tile (full width, 96px, flooded per state, age in tabular numerals at bottom-left, state glyph top-right), the map as a full-bleed tile, then the ordered stop list as large text rows. Desktop (1440): the same as a panorama, the blue rail on the left, headline and search in the left 40% of the plate, results/map on the right, light gutters 32px.

Signature interaction: the state tile is a live tile — every accepted sample flips it (rotateX, 600ms, exponential ease-out) to reveal the new age; press tilts any tile toward the finger; page content enters with one turnstile sweep from the left edge. Reduced motion removes flips and turnstile, keeps colour changes.

FORM: Challenger from the digital-design canon — Metro typographic tiles — chosen by the user over the assigned notice-board form (index 5 of the ordered list). Seed key f74a5608; challenger choice recorded with --chosen digital-design-canon-metro-typographic-tiles. Palette re-pinned by the owner on 12 Sep 2026 from a screenshot of the university's existing app (blue field, white cards, cool-grey ground, red errors, round badge logo): colour and logo only, on the owner's explicit instruction — structure, type, motion and the Firebase sign-in flow unchanged. Folded raises: nothing labelled twice; solid block sections with deep gutters; a distinct stamp form per tracking state; consequence + confirm before every irreversible action.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
